General Nutrition Guidelines

A balanced diet is essential for maintaining a healthy thyroid. Here are general guidelines:

  • Consume adequate iodine, which is crucial for thyroid hormone production (e.g., iodized salt, seafood, dairy).
  • Include selenium-rich foods like Brazil nuts, tuna, and eggs to protect the thyroid gland from oxidative stress.
  • Ensure sufficient zinc intake through meats, legumes, and seeds to support thyroid hormone synthesis.
  • Avoid excessive consumption of soy and raw goitrogenic vegetables (like cabbage and broccoli) if you have an iodine deficiency.

General Exercise Recommendations

Regular physical activity supports overall well-being and metabolic health:

  • Engage in at least 150 minutes of moderate aerobic activity or 75 minutes of vigorous aerobic activity a week.
  • Incorporate strength training exercises at least two days a week to boost metabolism.
  • Practice stress-reducing activities like yoga or tai chi, which can be beneficial, especially for autoimmune thyroid conditions.
  • Always consult your healthcare provider before starting any new exercise regimen.
